Our chapter

Chartered in 2024, and still filling the room

The NCMA MetroMD Chapter serves contract management professionals in the Maryland metropolitan area. We were founded to give the Maryland acquisition community its own place to meet — a hub for industry collaboration, professional development, and mentorship, empowering contracting professionals to connect, grow, and lead across government and industry.

What we do

Monthly dinner meetings with a speaker, virtual training sessions, certification study support and a kick-off each spring. Programming is planned by the board and shaped by what members ask for.

Who belongs here

Federal contracting officers and specialists, program and project staff, small business owners, and anyone who touches acquisition in Maryland. First-timers are welcome at any dinner — you do not have to be a member to come.

How to get involved

Come to a meeting, then take a committee seat. Members run study groups, edit the newsletter, host speakers and help with sponsorship. Say the word and the board will find you a place.

Why join NCMA

Industry Connections

Drive higher performance by attending events and education sessions which bring government and industry together to engage and learn from each other.

Certification

Professional designations of distinction, NCMA certifications carry the respect of your peers in the profession.

Events

Monthly meetings, talks and training opportunities.

Peer Networking

Learn from peers, ask questions, share ideas and discuss challenges.

Publications

Magazines, professional journals and NCMA books to help you stay ahead of the industry.

Training

Live or “on demand,” learn from subject matter experts about the “hottest” topics in the industry.

Professional credentials

Certifications

The CCCM, CFCM, CPCM, and CCMA are certifications awarded to candidates who meet rigorous standards, including experience, education, training, and knowledge. They are professional designations of distinction, and carry the respect of their peers in the profession.

Find out more about NCMA’s certification programs

CPCM

Certified Professional Contracts Manager

A CPCM certification demonstrates that you have met NCMA’s highest standards for education, training, and experience, and have demonstrated your knowledge of the contract management competencies in the Contract Management Body of Knowledge. It is NCMA’s senior and most prestigious certification.

CFCM

Certified Federal Contracts Manager

A CFCM certification validates your education, training, experience and your knowledge of the Federal Acquisition Regulation.

CCCM

Certified Commercial Contracts Manager

A CCCM certification validates your education, training, experience and your knowledge of the Uniform Commercial Code.

CCMA

Certified Contract Management Associate

The CCMA is an ANAB-Accredited, entry-level certification based on the ANSI-Approved Contract Management Standard™ and designed to assure new professionals and decision-makers that the right knowledge has been achieved to practice contract management.
